How to use

  • Rules: 15 points per set, win by 2. Deuce from 14–14. Match series is first to 2, 3, or 4 (default first to 2).
  • At 0–0 pick the first-serve team (name, disc, or left/right button).
  • Short tap on the big number: +1. Long press: −1.
  • Android APK during a rally: left click = left side +1, right click = right side +1, long press = −1.
  • Change ends after a set. Undo restores the previous state.
  • Simple mode shows large scores and sets only. Turn it on after first serve is chosen.
  • Settings: board look, OFL font, and score reading. Stored on this device only.
  • Cyber and ring looks tilt the side cards. Arena and broadcast add card outlines. No camera and no table-tennis venue photos.
  • Score reading uses device TTS (or the browser voice on the web). No table-tennis MP3 pack and no mic commands.
  • On Android the board advertises the same BLE score packet used by the existing Score watch app. No camera or microphone permission.
  • Scores stay on this device only (jokgu_scoreboard_state_v1).
  • Fonts: SIL OFL — Noto Sans KR, Jua, Do Hyeon, Orbitron. No GPL libraries.
