How to use

  • Rules: 15 points per set, win by 2. Deuce from 14–14. Match series is first to 2, 3, or 4 (default first to 2).
  • At 0–0 pick the first-serve team (name, discs 1–3, or left/right button). Disc 4 is the bench.
  • When the receiving team scores they take the serve and rotate positions 1–3. Consecutive points by the serving team do not rotate.
  • Short tap on the big number: +1. Long press: −1. There is no −1 button on the board.
  • Android APK during a rally: left click = left side +1, right click = right side +1, long press = −1.
  • Change ends after a set. Undo restores the previous state.
  • New match sits in the center set column. Undo, Simple, Settings, and Help stay on one row under the board.
  • Simple mode shows large scores and sets only. The bottom row keeps Undo and Full view. Turn it on after first serve is chosen.
  • The serving player disc uses a gold fill. Disc 4 is the bench.
  • Settings: language (한국어 / English / 中文), board look, OFL font, and score reading. Stored on this device only.
  • Board looks reuse Score colors and frames. No venue PNGs and no camera. Cyber look still lets you tap scores and discs.
  • Score reading uses device TTS (or the browser voice on the web). No table-tennis MP3 pack and no mic commands.
  • On Android the board advertises the same BLE score packet used by the existing Score watch app. No camera or microphone permission.
  • Scores stay on this device only (jokgu_scoreboard_state_v1).
  • Fonts: SIL OFL — Noto Sans KR, Jua, Do Hyeon, Orbitron. No GPL libraries.
